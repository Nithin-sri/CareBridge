Breaking the Stigma

It's OK to Ask for Help

Mental health affects 1 in 4 people every year. Seeking support is a sign of strength, not weakness.

😰

Feeling Anxious or Worried

Persistent worry, panic attacks, or feeling overwhelmed? Our counsellors can help you manage and reduce anxiety.

😔

Feeling Low or Depressed

Loss of interest, low mood, or feeling hopeless? You deserve support — and things can get better with the right help.

😤

Stress & Burnout

Work pressure, family stress, or feeling exhausted? We offer practical tools to help you regain balance and energy.

💔

Grief & Loss

Losing someone or something important is incredibly hard. Our compassionate team supports you through the grieving process.

😴

Sleep Problems

Poor sleep affects everything. We can help you identify the root causes and develop healthy sleep strategies.

🤝

Relationship Difficulties

Struggling with relationships, isolation, or loneliness? We offer both individual and couples support.

Individual Support

One-to-One Counselling

Our qualified counsellors provide a safe, confidential space where you can talk openly about anything that's troubling you — without fear of judgement.

Sessions are completely free and typically last 50 minutes. You can book a single session or an ongoing series depending on your needs.

  • BACP-qualified counsellors
  • 100% confidential
  • No GP referral needed
  • Flexible appointment times
  • In-person and telephone options
  • Available in multiple languages

Peer Support

Group Therapy Sessions

Sometimes sharing with others who truly understand makes all the difference. Our small, therapist-led group sessions create a safe community where healing happens together.

Current groups include: Anxiety Support, Depression Recovery, Grief & Loss, and General Wellbeing. New groups are added regularly based on demand.

  • Therapist-facilitated sessions
  • Small, intimate groups (6–8 people)
  • Shared experience & mutual support
  • Multiple group types available
  • Ongoing or drop-in options

Mindfulness & Relaxation

Mindfulness Workshops

Sessions are suitable for complete beginners. No experience needed — just come with an open mind and comfortable clothing.

  • Breathing & relaxation techniques
  • Body scan meditation
  • Stress reduction strategies
  • Sleep improvement practices
  • Mats & resources provided

If you or someone you know is in crisis, please reach out immediately. You don't have to face this alone — trained crisis workers are available around the clock.

Our Crisis Line +44 20 1234 5678
Samaritans (Free) 116 123
Crisis Text Line Text HELLO to 85258

Signs Someone May Need Help

  • Talking about wanting to die or self-harm
  • Withdrawing from friends and family
  • Giving away possessions
  • Extreme mood changes
  • Increased alcohol or drug use
  • Expressing feelings of hopelessness

If you notice these signs in someone, reach out to them with kindness and encourage them to seek help. In an emergency, always call 999.
