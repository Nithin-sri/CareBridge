Blood Pressure

Blood Pressure Check

High blood pressure often has no symptoms — it's known as the "silent killer". A quick 5-minute check could save your life. Our trained health staff will check your reading, explain what it means, and advise on next steps if needed.

  • Takes under 5 minutes
  • Walk-in — no appointment needed
  • Results explained clearly
  • GP referral if reading is high
  • Available to all adults 18+
Mon–Fri, 9am–4pm Free Health Suite, 1st Floor

BMI & Weight Assessment

Our BMI and weight assessment provides a clear picture of your healthy weight range and is paired with personalised nutrition and lifestyle advice from one of our health advisors.

  • Height, weight & BMI measurement
  • Waist circumference check
  • Personalised healthy weight advice
  • Goal-setting support
  • Referral to nutrition sessions

Blood Sugar

Blood Sugar Testing

Diabetes affects millions of people in the UK, many of whom don't know they have it. Our simple finger-prick test gives you an instant blood sugar reading, with guidance on what it means and what to do next.

  • Quick finger-prick test
  • Instant results
  • Type 2 diabetes risk assessment
  • Lifestyle advice included
  • GP referral if needed
Wed & Fri, 9am–2pm Free

Cholesterol Screening

High cholesterol increases your risk of heart disease and stroke. Our full lipid panel test checks your total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, and triglycerides — with expert advice on what the results mean for you.

  • Full lipid panel included
  • Heart disease risk assessment
  • Diet & lifestyle advice
  • GP referral if needed
  • Results explained clearly
