Gentle Exercise

Seated Exercise Classes

Our chair-based exercise classes are specifically designed for older adults and are suitable for all fitness levels — including those with limited mobility. Each class is led by a qualified instructor who ensures everyone exercises safely and comfortably.

  • Chair-based exercises — no floor work
  • Improves strength & flexibility
  • Reduces falls risk
  • Qualified instructors
  • All fitness levels welcome
  • Light refreshments provided
Mon, Wed & Fri, 10am Free Wellbeing Suite, 1st Floor

Memory Café

Memory Café

Our Memory Café is a warm, welcoming weekly gathering for people living with dementia and their carers. It's a chance to socialise, enjoy activities, and find peer support in a relaxed, compassionate environment.

  • Weekly meetups every Tuesday
  • Carer-friendly — bring a loved one
  • Themed activities each week
  • Tea, coffee & light refreshments
  • Dementia-friendly environment
  • Carer support groups available
Tuesdays, 10am–12pm Free

Mobility

Mobility Support

Led by qualified physiotherapists, our mobility sessions help older adults maintain their independence, improve balance, and reduce the risk of falls through personalised exercises and expert advice.

  • Physiotherapist-led sessions
  • Falls prevention programme
  • Personalised exercise plans
  • Balance & coordination training
  • Walking aid assessment
Tue & Thu, 11am Free

Social Club

Elderly Social Club

Loneliness and isolation can seriously affect health. Our social club brings older adults together for regular activities, outings, and events designed to spark joy, build friendships, and create a true sense of belonging.

  • Weekly social gatherings
  • Board games, quizzes & crafts
  • Monthly day trips & outings
  • Music & dance sessions
  • Seasonal events & celebrations
Thurs & Fri, 1pm–4pm Free
