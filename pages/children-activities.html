Creative Arts

Arts & Crafts Club

Our weekly arts and crafts sessions let children explore their creativity through painting, drawing, sculpture, collage, and more. All materials are provided and sessions are led by qualified art teachers in a fun, encouraging environment.

  • Ages 5–12 welcome
  • All materials provided
  • Qualified art teachers
  • Themed sessions each week
  • Take-home creations
  • Small group sizes
Sat & Sun, 10am–12pm Free Art Room, 1st Floor

Swimming

Children's Swimming Lessons

Our structured swimming programme caters for all levels — from complete beginners to advanced young swimmers. Classes are small to ensure every child gets individual attention from our qualified instructors.

  • All skill levels catered for
  • ASA-qualified instructors
  • Small group sizes (max 8)
  • Progress badges awarded
  • Separate learner pool available
Mon, Wed & Fri, 4pm–6pm £2 per lesson

After-School

After-School Club

A safe, fun, and stimulating environment for children aged 5–11 after school. Our experienced staff provide homework support, healthy snacks, and a range of activities to keep children engaged and happy until parents arrive.

  • Monday to Friday, 3pm–6pm
  • Homework help provided
  • Healthy snacks included
  • Arts, crafts & games
  • Outdoor play when weather permits
  • DBS-checked staff
Mon–Fri, 3pm–6pm Free Children's Wing

Soft Play

Indoor Soft Play Zone

Our massive multi-level soft play structure is a child's paradise — with slides, ball pits, climbing frames, tunnels, and a dedicated toddler section. All equipment is safety-checked daily and the area is fully supervised by trained staff.

  • Suitable for ages 1–8
  • Dedicated toddler section
  • Fully supervised at all times
  • Daily safety checks
  • Open 7 days a week
  • Party bookings available
9am–6pm daily £1.50 per child Children's Wing, Ground Floor
