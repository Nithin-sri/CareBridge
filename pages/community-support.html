Housing

Housing Advice

Whether you're facing eviction, struggling with repairs, looking for social housing, or unsure of your tenancy rights — our housing advisors are here to guide you through the process step by step.

  • Tenancy rights & responsibilities
  • Eviction prevention support
  • Social housing applications
  • Emergency accommodation referrals
  • Landlord dispute guidance
  • Council housing support
Mon–Fri, 9am–4pm Free Community Suite, Ground Floor

Benefits

Benefits Guidance

Navigating the benefits system can be confusing and overwhelming. Our trained advisors help you understand what you're entitled to, help you apply, and support you through any challenges or appeals.

  • Universal Credit support
  • PIP & disability benefits
  • Tax credits guidance
  • Form filling assistance
  • Appeals & complaints help
  • Benefits calculator access
Mon, Wed & Fri, 10am–3pm Free

Food Support

Food Bank Referrals

If you or your family are struggling to afford food, we provide discreet, compassionate referrals to local food banks and community pantries. There is no shame in asking for help — we're here to support you.

  • Same-day referrals available
  • Completely discreet & confidential
  • Links to 5 local food banks
  • Community pantry access
  • Ongoing support available
  • No judgement — ever
Walk-in welcome Free

Inclusion

Social Inclusion Programme

No one should feel isolated or excluded. Our social inclusion programme helps people from all backgrounds connect with their community through social events, volunteer opportunities, language support, and cultural activities.

  • Weekly community social events
  • English language support classes
  • Volunteering opportunities
  • Cultural celebration events
  • Interpreter support available
  • Befriending scheme
