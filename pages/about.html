What Drives Us

Our Purpose

Everything we do is guided by three core principles — our mission, vision, and values.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a welcoming, inclusive community space where every person — regardless of age, background, or ability — can access the health, wellbeing, and social support they need to live a happier, healthier life.

🔭

Our Vision

A thriving community where no one feels alone, excluded, or without support — where mental and physical wellbeing are equally valued, and where everyone has the opportunity to flourish.

💎

Our Values

Compassion, inclusivity, dignity, and community. We believe in treating every person with respect and warmth — meeting them wherever they are in their wellbeing journey.

Our Story

How It All Started

The Community Wellbeing Hub was founded in 2018 by a group of local health professionals and community volunteers who saw a growing need for accessible, affordable wellbeing support in East London.

What started as a small drop-in centre offering mental health counselling and basic health checks has grown into one of the most comprehensive community wellbeing centres in London — serving over 4,200 members across all age groups.

Today, we offer a wide range of services including mental health support, free health screenings, fitness facilities, family services, elderly care, children's activities, and community assistance — all in one welcoming space.

"We started small, but our belief was always big — that everyone in our community deserves support, dignity, and the chance to thrive."

— Dr. Amara Osei, Founder

Our Journey

How We've Grown

From a small drop-in centre to a thriving community hub — here's our story.

2018

The Beginning

Founded by local health professionals with a vision to create an inclusive wellbeing space for East London.

2019

Growing Fast

Expanded to 500 members. Added fitness facilities, swimming pool, and family services.

2020

Supporting the Community

During the pandemic, we provided emergency food support, online counselling, and community assistance to over 1,000 local households.

2022

Award Winning

Awarded the London Community Wellbeing Award for outstanding contribution to public health and social inclusion.

2025

Today & Beyond

4,200+ members, 18 facilities, 120+ weekly activities. Continuing to grow and serve our community every day.

Our Core Values

Everything we do is guided by these principles — every day, for every person.

💜

Compassion

We approach every person with genuine warmth, empathy, and care — no matter who they are or what they're going through.

🌍

Inclusivity

We welcome everyone — all ages, backgrounds, abilities, and identities. Our doors are open to the whole community.
